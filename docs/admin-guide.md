# Using the dashboard

For the Yuri Perfections team. Sign in at **yuriperfections.com/login**. Everything you save shows on the
website straight away (refresh the website page to see it).

## Inbox: enquiries from the website

Every message someone sends through the contact form lands here. A gold dot and bold name mean **new**; the
number next to **Inbox** in the menu is how many are new.

- **Open** a message to read it. It is marked read.
- **Reply by email**, **Call** or **WhatsApp** from the buttons in the message.
- **Notes** are for the team only (for example "quote sent Tuesday").
- **Archive** finished ones to keep the inbox short; they stay under the *Archived* tab.
  **Mark unread** puts one back as new.
- **Search** by name, email, phone or words in the message. **Filters** picks a date range.
- **Excel** or **CSV** downloads every message matching what you are looking at (all pages, not just the one
  on screen).

## Visitors

How many people opened the website, how often, from where (Instagram, Google, WhatsApp…), from which countries
and on which devices. Pick 7 days, 30 days, 90 days or 12 months at the top. Tip: add `?utm_source=instagram` to
the website link you post on Instagram (or `whatsapp`, `facebook`, `tiktok`) so those visits are named
correctly; apps often hide where a click came from.

## Before anything final, the dashboard asks

Deleting, hiding or showing something on the website, saving a project, saving Site settings, archiving an
enquiry and changing someone's role or access all open a short "Are you sure?" box first. Cancel or Esc leaves
everything as it was.

## Lists

Every list shows 5 rows at a time; change it with **Rows** at the bottom (up to 100) and move with the arrows.
The search box and the page you are on stay in the address, so a refresh or a shared link opens the same view.

## Projects

Your portfolio. Each project has one or more **before and after pairs** (the same spot photographed before
and after the work).

1. **New project**. Give it a title; the web address fills itself in.
2. Pick a **category** (add categories under *Categories* first).
3. Add **Completed on**: the newest work shows first on the website.
4. For each room or area, add a **pair**: name it (e.g. *Living room*), then a **before** and an **after**
   photo. Click the box or drag a photo onto it. Photos from a phone are fine; they are resized for you.
5. The **first pair is the cover** (its photos show on cards). Use the arrows to change the order.
6. **Live** shows it on the website; switch it off to hide it without deleting.
   **Featured** puts it first in the home page portfolio.
7. **Create project**. Then *View on website* to see it.

The star and the switch in the project list feature or hide a project in one click.

## Hero slides, Services, Categories

Short lists shown in the order you set with the **up and down arrows**. Each row has a **Live** switch,
**Edit** and **Delete**; each form also has a **Show on website** switch, so something can be prepared hidden
and switched on later. (Testimonials are switched off for now, as on the old site.)

- **Hero slides**: the large photos at the top of the home page. Wide (landscape) photos look best.
- **Services**: title, a sentence or two, a photo and an icon. Their titles also fill the *Service* list in
  the contact form.
- **Categories**: the filter buttons on the portfolio page. Renaming one renames it on all its projects. A
  category that still has projects cannot be deleted: move the projects first.

## Site settings

Phone, WhatsApp, email, address, map link, opening hours, social media links, the home page intro text and
the About section (heading, text, ticked highlights, figures, photos). The bar at the top jumps between the
sections. **Figures** are the numbers under the About text: a number (e.g. 200+) and its label (e.g. Projects).
Social icons always show in the footer; the ones with a link filled in here open it. Save with the button at the
bottom.

## Users (super admin only)

- **Add user**: name, email, role, and a starting password (one is suggested). Send it to them privately;
  they can change it under **My account**.
- **Admin** manages the website and the inbox. **Super admin** can also add and remove users.
- **Access** off stops someone signing in to the dashboard without deleting them.
- **Password** sets a new one for someone who is locked out.
- You cannot remove your own super admin access or delete yourself (so the business is never locked out).

## My account

See your details and change your password. Forgot it? On the sign-in page choose **Forgot password?** and open
the emailed link on the same device.
